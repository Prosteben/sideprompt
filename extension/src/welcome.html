Your prompts, one click away in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more.

3 steps to get going

Pin the icon. Click the puzzle piece 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar and pin PromptVault. Or just press Alt+P.
Open an AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) and click the PromptVault icon – the side panel opens next to the page.
Click any prompt → Insert. Prompts with {{variables}} ask you to fill in the blanks first. On unsupported pages the prompt is copied instead.

Pro tips

• Select text on any web page → right-click → Save selection to PromptVault.
• Search with #tag, press / to jump to search, N for a new prompt.
• Use {{topic|default value}} for pre-filled variables and {{date}} for today's date.
• Right-click a folder chip to rename or delete it.

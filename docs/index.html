Questions

Where are my prompts stored?

Locally, in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. SidePrompt has no servers. With Pro you can turn on sync, which uses Chrome's own sync storage tied to your Google account, still without any server of ours.

Which AI chats are supported?

One-click insert works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity, among others. On any other website the prompt is copied to your clipboard instead.

Does it read my conversations?

No. The content script only writes the prompt you clicked into the chat input. It does not read page content, and there are no analytics or tracking.

What happens when I hit the 15-prompt limit?

Everything keeps working. You can still edit, insert and organize your existing prompts. To add more, upgrade to Pro once for $9.99.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay once, use it forever, including all future updates. No subscription and no account, the license is tied to your browser install and can be restored with the e-mail you used at checkout.

Can I move my prompts to another computer?

Export to JSON is free. Import is a Pro feature, or simply turn on Pro sync and your library follows your Google account across Chrome browsers.

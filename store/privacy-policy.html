PromptNook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

PromptNook is a browser extension that stores your AI prompts and inserts them into AI chat websites. It is built to be private by design.

What data PromptNook stores

  • Your prompts, folders, tags and settings. These are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. If you enable the optional Sync across devices feature (Pro), the same data is stored in Chrome's sync storage, which Google synchronises between browsers signed in to your Google account. In both cases the data never reaches any server operated by us – we do not operate any servers.
  • License status. A single boolean (paid / not paid) and the time it was last checked, stored locally.

What PromptNook does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your prompts, browsing history, page content or any personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • It does not read the content of the AI chat websites. The content script only writes the prompt you chose into the chat input box when you click Insert.
  • It does not execute remote code.

Payments (Pro)

Pro purchases are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you open the payment page, ExtensionPay generates an anonymous installation key so it can tell the extension whether a purchase has been made. Your e‑mail address and payment details are handled by ExtensionPay/Stripe under their own privacy policies and are never visible to the extension or to us.

Permissions

  • storage – save your prompts and settings.
  • sidePanel – show the prompt library in Chrome's side panel.
  • contextMenus – the "Save selection to PromptNook" right‑click item.
  • clipboardWrite – copy a prompt to your clipboard when the current page has no chat box.
  • alarms – re-check the license status once a day.
  • Access to specific AI chat websites (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, …) – needed to insert the prompt into the chat box on those sites only.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. You can also delete everything from Settings → Delete all prompts. Sync data can be removed by disabling sync in Settings and using "Delete all".
